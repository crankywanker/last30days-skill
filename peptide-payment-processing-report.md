## 1. Why Peptides Are High-Risk

### Regulatory Gray Zone
- Peptides sold as "research chemicals" with "not for human consumption" disclaimers are legally ineffective per FDA
- FDA classifies peptides marketed to treat/cure conditions as **unapproved drugs**
- The disclaimer "for research only" is viewed skeptically by regulators and processors alike

### Mainstream Processors Will Ban You
- **Stripe, PayPal, Square, Shopify Payments** all explicitly ban peptides
- Accounts are typically frozen **without warning** after 1-4 weeks of activity
- Funds can be held for **180+ days** (timer resets with each chargeback)

## 6. Typical Terms & Fees

### Rates
- **Transaction fees**: 3.5% - 6.5% + $0.20-$0.35 per transaction
- **Monthly fees**: $25 - $100
- **Setup fees**: $0 - $1,500 (offshore accounts higher)
- **Chargeback fees**: $25 - $50 each

### Rolling Reserve
- **Typical**: 5-15% of daily batch held
- **Release**: Month 7 (rolling - funds from month 1 released in month 7)
- **Clean history**: May reduce to 3-6 months or lower percentage

### Approval Requirements
- **Minimum 6 months** in business (startups generally rejected)
- **LegitScript certification** (increasingly mandatory)
- **U.S.-based** (many won't ship international)
- **Clean history**: No FDA warning letters, no negative press
- **Documentation**: EIN, government ID, bank letter, 3-6 months processing history, supplier invoices

## 9. Actionable Recommendations

### Before You Start
1. **Get LegitScript certified first** - this is now table stakes
2. **Have 6+ months operating history** before applying
3. **Prepare extensive documentation** upfront
4. **Budget for higher costs**: 5-7% effective rate is realistic

### When Choosing a Processor
1. **Never pay large upfront fees in crypto**
2. **Verify physical addresses** and certifications independently
3. **Ask for references** from other peptide merchants
4. **Read the contract** for early termination fees ($295-$595 common)
5. **Understand the rolling reserve** terms explicitly
6. **Consider multiple accounts** for load balancing

### Ongoing Operations
1. Keep chargebacks under 1%
2. Maintain "research only" labeling and compliance
3. Monitor regulatory changes (VIRP, BRAM, FDA)
4. Build relationship with account manager
5. Have backup payment methods ready
