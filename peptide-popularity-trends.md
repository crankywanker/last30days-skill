# Peptide Popularity & Demand Trends Analysis

## Executive Summary

This document analyzes which peptides are driving consumer demand, what people are discussing in communities, pricing benchmarks, and emerging trends. Use this to inform product selection and inventory prioritization.

**Key Findings:**
- BPC-157 is the single most popular peptide among biohackers
- GLP-1 peptides (semaglutide, tirzepatide) dominate weight loss category
- "Wolverine Stack" (BPC-157 + TB-500) is the most requested combination
- Imports of peptides from China doubled in 2025 ($328M vs $164M in 2024)
- Online peptide promotion increased 8x from 2022-2024

## Tier 1: Highest Demand Peptides

### 1. BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound)

**Demand Level**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Highest

**Why It's #1:**
> "BPC-157 anchors popular peptide stacks and is the most popular among biohackers today."

**Primary Use Cases:**
- Injury recovery (tendons, ligaments, muscles)
- Gut health and healing
- Joint pain relief
- Post-surgical recovery

**Community Discussion Themes:**
| Topic | Discussion Frequency |
|-------|---------------------|
| Soft tissue injury healing | Very High |
| Tendon/ligament repair | Very High |
| Gut health improvement | High |
| Joint pain (runner's knee, shoulder) | High |
| Post-workout recovery | Medium |

**Research Status:**
- NO FDA-approved indication
- Category 2 bulk drug substance (2023) - cannot be compounded commercially
- Evidence primarily from rodent studies
- No large-scale human clinical trials

**Pricing Benchmarks:**
| Size | Price Range | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| 5mg vial | $30-$70 | Wide variance based on quality |
| 10mg vial | $50-$120 | Premium vendors higher |

**8-Week Cycle Cost**: $150-$250

**Safety Concerns:**
- Angiogenesis (new blood vessel growth) - theoretical cancer concern
- Caution advised for TBI patients
- No long-term human safety data

**Regulatory Status:**
- Temporarily banned by WADA in 2022 (not currently listed)
- FDA Category 2 (2023)

### 3. Semaglutide (GLP-1)

**Demand Level**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Highest (Weight Loss Category)

**Why Dominant:**
- FDA-approved (Ozempic, Wegovy)
- 15% average weight loss in trials

**Regulatory Caution:**
- FDA actively targeting research chemical sellers
- Warning letters issued December 2024 to multiple vendors
- "Research only" defense not holding up

**Pricing:**
| Source | Monthly Cost |
|--------|--------------|
| Prescription (Ozempic) | $900+ without insurance |
| Research chemical | $50-$150 |

### 4. Tirzepatide (Dual GLP-1/GIP)

**Demand Level**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Very High

**Why Growing:**

- ~20% average weight loss (better than semaglutide)
- Dual mechanism (GLP-1 + GIP)

**Market Position:**
- Zepbound segment predicted for significant growth

**Regulatory Status:**
- FDA approved for weight loss (November 2023)
- Research chemical versions under FDA scrutiny

### 5. CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin (Growth Hormone Stack)

**Demand Level**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ High

**Why Combined:**
> "CJC-1295 takes 1-4 hours to reach peak serum, while Ipamorelin works much quicker. The combination ensures rapid onset with Ipamorelin and lasting effects with CJC-1295."

**Use Cases:**

- Anti-aging
- Fat loss
- Muscle preservation
- Sleep improvement

**Mechanism:**
- CJC-1295: GHRH analog (long-acting, 5.8-8.1 day half-life)
- Ipamorelin: Selective GHRP (doesn't raise cortisol/prolactin)

**Pricing:**
| Product | Price Range |
|---------|-------------|
| CJC-1295 (2mg) | $30-$50 |
| Ipamorelin (5mg) | $35-$55 |
| Combination vials | $50-$80 |

**Regulatory Status:**
- Prohibited by WADA at all times
- Not FDA approved
- CJC-1295 clinical trial discontinued after patient death

## Tier 2: Strong Demand Peptides

### 6. GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

**Demand Level**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ High

**Why Popular:**
> "A lot of men in their 30s and 40s use it to recover AND look better."

**Use Cases:**
- Skin rejuvenation (anti-aging)
- Hair growth
- Wound healing
- Collagen production

**Key Research:**
- Increases collagen production up to 70% (lab studies)
- Influences 4,000+ genes involved in tissue repair
- Plasma levels decline with age (200ng/ml at 20 → 80ng/ml at 60)

**Forms Available:**
- Injectable
- Topical (serums, creams)
- Scalp solutions

**Pricing:**
| Form | Price Range |
|------|-------------|
| Injectable (5mg) | $25-$45 |
| Topical serums | $40-$100 |

**Safety Profile:**
- Long history of safe use
- Naturally occurring
- Active at nanomolar concentration

## Demand Signals & Trends

### Rising Trends (2025-2026)

1. **GLP-1 Alternatives**
   - Retatrutide demand surging despite not being approved
   - Users seeking stronger weight loss

2. **Oral Peptides**
   - 5-Amino-1MQ growing due to oral availability
   - AI-assisted peptide design for oral stability

3. **Mitochondrial Peptides**
   - MOTS-c gaining traction in longevity community
   - Combined with NAD+ protocols

4. **Combination Products**
   - Pre-made blends increasing in popularity
   - "Stacks" sell at premium

5. **Microdosing GLP-1s**
   - Users experimenting with low-dose protocols
   - Interest in compulsive behavior reduction

### Declining/Stable Trends

1. **Standalone GH secretagogues** - Being replaced by combinations
2. **Single-peptide protocols** - Users prefer stacks
3. **Injectable-only mindset** - Oral/topical gaining interest

### Demographic Insights

| Demographic | Primary Interest |
|-------------|------------------|
| Men 30-40 | Recovery + aesthetics (BPC-157, GHK-Cu) |

| Women 30-50 | Weight loss (GLP-1s) |
| Athletes | Recovery stacks |
